# Privacy Policy for AskBubble

Last updated: May 25, 2026

AskBubble is a browser extension that allows users to select text and interact with AI directly on webpages.

## Information We Collect

AskBubble may process:

* Selected text content that users explicitly choose
* User prompts entered into the extension
* Temporary interaction data required for AI responses

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

## How Data Is Used

The collected information is used solely for:

* Generating AI responses
* Improving extension functionality
* Maintaining service reliability

## Data Sharing

AskBubble does not share user data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

If AI services are used through external APIs, selected content may be transmitted securely to those providers only for generating responses.

## Data Storage

AskBubble does not permanently store personal browsing history or webpage content unless explicitly stated.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect transmitted information and minimize unnecessary data collection.

## Changes

This privacy policy may be updated periodically.
